# Tanach audio credits

Chapter recordings: the Hebrew Bible read by **Abraham Shmuelof** (1913–1994).
Original tapes digitized by Audio Scriptures International; chapter MP3s via
the public mirrors of the Academy of Ancient Languages / grahame/Schmueloff
repositories. Distributed by eBible.org under the
**Creative Commons BY-NC-ND 4.0** license.

Usage here: the original chapter files are served **unmodified** (no cutting,
no re-encoding) for non-commercial educational use, with attribution shown in
the UI ("קריינות: אברהם שמואלוף"). Verse-level playback is implemented by
seeking within the original files using our own timestamp metadata
(audio-map.json, generated by scripts/asr_align_verses.py) — the recordings
themselves are not altered.
